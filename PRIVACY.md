# Privacy policy: AI Stack Version Check

Last updated: 2026-10-14. Publisher: MV2 of Munim, Inc.

**The extension collects no personal data and sends nothing to Munim, Inc. or anyone else.**

What it does with data:

- **Advisory feed.** It downloads a public file,
  `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/munimtechnologies/ai-stack-advisories/main/advisories.json`, from
  GitHub. As with any web request, GitHub sees your IP address. No other information is sent.
- **Read from this tab.** Only when you click this button, the extension asks the page open in
  that tab for its own version number (for example `/api/version` on your Ollama server). The
  request goes to that page's address only. The answer is used to fill in the version field and is
  not sent anywhere.
- **Stored in your browser.** The downloaded feed, the time it was downloaded, and the last app
  and version you checked are kept in the extension's local storage on your computer. Removing the
  extension deletes them.

There are no analytics, ads, accounts, cookies set by the extension, or remote code.
